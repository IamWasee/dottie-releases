      Notes · a note from dottie.md

      hi, I'm the dot up top.

      Computers got powerful and somehow still make you hunt for the button. I live in your notch so you can just say what you want and keep your hands where they were.

      I'll point when you're learning, take over when you're busy, and hand the long jobs to my helpers while you get on with your day. I remember what we did together, so you don't have to explain twice.

      I only look at your screen when you hold the keys, I never type your passwords, and I ask before anything that sends, deletes or buys.

      dottie, in your notch
      trust

      Helpful, not nosy

      lookingOnly when you hold the keysA frame of your screen goes with your question. Nothing is watched in between unless you switch on screen memory yourself.
      askingAsks before it actsAnything that sends, deletes or buys asks you first. Helpers ask before they click or type in your apps.
      keysNo keys in the appAI keys live on Dottie's server, never inside the app on your Mac.
      meetingsNotes, not recordingsMeeting mode is off until you start it, and the audio itself is never kept. Only the notes are.
